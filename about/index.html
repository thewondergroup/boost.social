Why we built Boost Social

We're the team behind The Wonder Group, a London agency that has grown Michelin-starred restaurants, large hotel groups and international lifestyle brands. Over the years, the same thing kept happening: brilliant independent venues would come to us needing help, but a full-service agency retainer was more than they could justify.

So we built Boost Social. It takes what actually works in hospitality marketing — consistent content, local ads, email and now AI search — and delivers it as a simple, affordable subscription. We keep it cheap by automating the busywork and sourcing content through a creator network, so the quality stays high and the price stays low.

What we believe

Consistency beats brilliance

Part of something bigger
