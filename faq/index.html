Frequently asked questions

What is Boost Social?

Boost Social is an affordable, always-on marketing subscription for hospitality businesses. We run your social media, ads, newsletters and AI search visibility for you from £500 a month, with no long contract. It's a Wonder Group company.

How much does it cost?

Social is £500/month, Social + Ads £800/month and Full Boost £999/month. Add-ons are Newsletters at £200, AI Boost at £99, and a Website from £250/month on a 12-month term.

What's included in the base plan?

Four posts a week, real creator photo and video, captions written in your brand voice, and scheduling across your channels — plus a monthly report.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Social, ads, newsletters and AI listings are month-to-month. Only the optional website has a 12-month minimum term.

Do you run the ads and pay for them?

We build and manage the ads. The ad spend itself is paid directly to the platforms on your own card, so there's no mark-up.

How does "get found by AI" work?

We make your business the clear, well-referenced answer when people ask AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI where to eat, drink or stay — so you're recommended by name.

Do you build websites?

Yes — a website designed to win bookings and be found on Google and AI, hosted and kept up to date, from £250/month on a 12-month term.

How much of my time does this take?

About fifteen minutes to onboard. After that we run everything, and you approve posts only if you want to.

Who is it for?

Independent and small hospitality businesses — restaurants, cafés, hotels, bars and pubs — that want a professional presence without agency prices.

How quickly can we start?

Usually within a week of onboarding.
