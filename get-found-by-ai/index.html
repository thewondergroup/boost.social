Frequently asked questions

What does "get found by AI" actually mean?

It means your business is recommended when people ask AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI for places to eat, drink or stay — so you appear in the answer itself, not just in a list of blue links.

How is this different from normal SEO?

Traditional SEO gets you ranked in Google's results. AI search optimisation gets you cited inside AI-generated answers, which is where a growing share of decisions now happen. We do both.

Can you guarantee I'll appear in ChatGPT?

No one can guarantee a specific AI answer — the models change constantly. What we can do is make your business the clearest, best-referenced option in your area, which is what those answers reward.
