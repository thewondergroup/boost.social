Frequently asked questions

Is ad spend included in the price?

No — the £800/month covers building and managing the ads. You pay the ad spend itself directly to the platforms, so there's no mark-up. Most venues start around £500/month in spend.

Which platforms do you advertise on?

Mainly Meta (Instagram and Facebook) and TikTok, using the content we already make for your organic posts.

How often do you change the ads?

We test and refresh the creative and targeting once a month, which keeps performance steady without over-managing.

Do I need the social media plan too?

Yes — ads work best alongside consistent posting, so advertising is added on top of social media management.

Can you prove it's working?

You get a monthly report in plain English: who we reached, and what the spend brought back in bookings and enquiries.
